Lash vs Seven · plan for the store

Chip shop

Every match pays out in chips. Chips buy looks: hats and outfits for your Ops, new things to throw, sticker packs, win celebrations, card backs and new characters. Nothing in the shop helps anyone win, and chips are never bought with money.

The currency

Chips, like a casino table

Each value has its own colour, the way real casino chips do: a thick ink rim, striped notches round the edge, the value in the middle. Tap one to flip it.

The shop

A page of its own

Opened from a tile on the homepage, next to Rivals and Stakes, and laid out like Ops’ room. Your stack of chips sits in the corner. Each item shows its price in chips; once bought it says Owned, and Wear puts it on. Whatever you wear goes into your room entry, the same way your Ops face does, so every phone in the match sees it.

Paying sends your chips flying out of the stack onto the item. Everyone starts with one free hat and one free throwable, so the shop makes sense from day one.

House rules

What the shop never does

No pay to win

No hints, undos or extra time. Everything is looks or fun.

No real money

Chips can’t be bought, cashed out or sent to someone else.

No betting chips

Stakes stay about real things between friends. Chips you could bet would make the app look like gambling, which matters for app stores and younger players. The casino look stays a look.

Under the hood

Where chips live

Players are anonymous accounts on their own phone, so a cleared browser or a new phone would lose everything. Phase one keeps chips in the database under the player’s id, with rules that cap how much one result can add. Phase two adds “Back up your chips” with Google sign-in, so chips and items move to a new phone.

Without a paid server, someone determined could edit their own count. For a friends app that’s fine: chips only buy looks, and nobody can spend them on anyone else.

Build order

One step at a time

  1. Chips

    The chip art, your stack on the homepage, payouts on every result screen with the clack, and the farming limits.

  2. Shop page

    Items, prices, Buy, Owned and Wear, laid out like Ops’ room.

  3. Wardrobe

    Hats, outfits and colours. Every character needs spots to hang them on, so this is the biggest drawing job.

  4. Throwables and sticker packs

    Quick wins: both systems already exist.

  5. Celebrations and score card flair
  6. Board and card skins

    One game at a time, Spar card backs first.

  7. Back up with Google

    Chips and items follow you to a new phone.

Still to decide
